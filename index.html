Chronix 隐私政策

生效日期：2026年3月24日 · 开发者：Fuze（support@fuze.app）

Chronix（以下简称「本应用」）是一款本地运行的节拍器、调音器与节拍检测工具。我们重视您的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理信息。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

本应用默认不收集、不上传、不向我们的服务器发送您的个人身份信息。应用功能主要在设备本地完成。

  • 麦克风音频：仅在您使用调音器或节拍检测功能时，用于实时分析音高/节拍。音频在设备本地处理，不会录制保存或上传。
  • 应用设置与预设：速度、拍号、声音、颜色主题、练习提醒等偏好保存在您的设备本地。
  • 通知：若您开启练习提醒，系统会在本地调度通知；我们不会因此获得您的通讯录或其他个人信息。

2. 我们不做什么

  • 不创建账户，不要求登录
  • 不出售、出租或交易用户数据
  • 不将麦克风音频用于广告、画像或云端分析
  • 不集成用于跨应用追踪的第三方广告标识 SDK（以当前版本为准）

3. 权限说明

  • 麦克风：调音器、节拍检测
  • 通知：练习提醒与系统播放控制相关提示（可选）
  • 后台音频：节拍器在切到后台时继续发声

您可随时在系统设置中撤销相应权限；撤销后相关功能将不可用，但不影响其他本地功能。

4. 数据存储与删除

偏好与预设存储于设备本地。卸载本应用将删除应用本地数据（以操作系统行为为准）。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用面向一般用户，不专门面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

6. 政策更新

我们可能不时更新本政策。更新后将在本页面发布新版本并调整生效日期。

7. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请邮件联系：support@fuze.app

Chronix Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 24, 2026 · Developer: Fuze (support@fuze.app)

Chronix (“the App”) is a locally running metronome, tuner, and beat-detection tool. This policy explains how information is handled.

1. Information We Process

The App does not collect, upload, or transmit personal identity information to our servers by default. Features run on your device.

  • Microphone audio: Used only while you use the tuner or beat detector for on-device pitch/tempo analysis. Audio is processed locally and is not recorded for storage or upload.
  • Settings and presets: Tempo, time signature, sounds, themes, practice reminders, and similar preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Notifications: If you enable practice reminders, notifications are scheduled locally by the system. We do not receive your contacts or other personal information for this purpose.

2. What We Do Not Do

  • No accounts or sign-in requirement
  • No selling, renting, or trading of user data
  • No use of microphone audio for advertising, profiling, or cloud analysis
  • No third-party advertising / cross-app tracking identifier SDKs in the current version

3. Permissions

  • Microphone: Tuner and beat detector
  • Notifications: Optional practice reminders and related system prompts
  • Background audio: Continues metronome playback when the App is in the background

You may revoke permissions in system settings at any time. Related features will stop working; other local features remain available.

4. Storage and Deletion

Preferences and presets are stored on device. Uninstalling the App removes its local data, subject to OS behavior.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@fuze.app
